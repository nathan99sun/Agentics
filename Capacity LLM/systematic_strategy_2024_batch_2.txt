===========================================
SYSTEMATIC TRADING RULES
===========================================

Data Exploration Summary:
- The average z-scores for bm, mom12m, and mve at December 2023 are approximately -0.05, -0.02, and -0.09 respectively.
- Extreme values for bm were found in firms with z-scores greater than 2.0.
- A test of complex conditions revealed that 39 out of 250 total firms met the condition of (bm > 2.0) OR (mom12m < -1.0).

BUY RULE:
if (bm > 2.0) OR (mom12m < -1.0):
    signal = BUY

SELL RULE:
if (bm < -2.0) OR (mom12m > 1.0):
    signal = SELL

HOLD RULE:
else:
    signal = HOLD

Rationale:
- BUY Rule: Firms with extremely high book-to-market values (undervalued) or very low 12-month momentum (poor performance) are considered potential buying opportunities.
- SELL Rule: Firms with extremely low book-to-market values (overvalued) or very high 12-month momentum (strong performance) are considered potential selling opportunities.
- These rules aim to capture opportunities based on value and momentum factors while maintaining a balanced approach with the HOLD rule.

Expected signal distribution:
- BUY: ~15.6% of firms
- SELL: To be determined based on the threshold for extreme values
