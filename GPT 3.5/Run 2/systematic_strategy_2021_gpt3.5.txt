===========================================
SYSTEMATIC TRADING RULES
===========================================

Data Exploration Summary:
- The data for December 2020 consists of 497 S&P 500 firms with standardized characteristics: mve (log firm size), bm (log book-to-market ratio), and mom12m (12-month momentum).
- Extreme values for mve were identified with z-score > 2.0, indicating firms with very large or very small market values.

BUY RULE:
if (bm > 1.0 AND mom12m > 0.5) OR mve > 2.0:
    signal = BUY

SELL RULE:
if bm < -1.0 AND mom12m < -1.0:
    signal = SELL

HOLD RULE:
else:
    signal = HOLD

Rationale:
- BUY Rule: Firms with high bm and mom12m values or extreme mve may indicate undervalued or strong-performing stocks.
- SELL Rule: Firms with low bm and mom12m values may suggest overvalued or weak-performing stocks.
- These rules aim to capture potential opportunities in undervalued and strong-performing stocks while avoiding overvalued or weak-performing stocks based on the characteristics observed in December 2020.
