SYSTEMATIC TRADING RULES
===========================================

Data Exploration Summary:
- The data for December 2021 includes three main standardized characteristics:
  - mve: log firm size
  - bm: log book-to-market ratio (value factor)
  - mom12m: 12-month momentum
- Extreme values for each characteristic will be explored to identify potential thresholds.
- Correlations between characteristics will be considered for rule development.

BUY RULE:
if bm > 1.45 AND mom12m > 1.25:
    signal = BUY

SELL RULE:
if bm < -1.35 OR mom12m < -1.15:
    signal = SELL

HOLD RULE:
else:
    signal = HOLD

Rationale:
- BUY Rule: We aim to BUY stocks with high book-to-market ratio (indicating undervaluation) and strong 12-month momentum. This strategy targets potentially undervalued stocks with positive momentum for potential future gains.
- SELL Rule: Stocks with very low book-to-market ratio or weak 12-month momentum are considered for SELL signals. This strategy helps in cutting losses or taking profits from stocks showing signs of overvaluation or weak performance.
- Expected Signal Distribution: The BUY rule is expected to identify value stocks with positive momentum, while the SELL rule aims to capture stocks showing signs of overvaluation or weak momentum.
