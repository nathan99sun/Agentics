==========================================

SYSTEMATIC TRADING RULES
==========================================

**Data Exploration Summary**

* Database: firm_data.db
* Table: firms
* Total rows: 269,255
* Date range: 1980-01-31 to 2024-12-31
* Standardization:
	+ 'mve' column: log(market value of equity)
	+ 'bm' column: log(book to market)
	+ 'mom12m' column: 12-month momentum
	+ ALL features are standardized: mean = 0, standard deviation = 1

**BUY Rule**

* Condition: (bm > 1.1 AND mom12m > 0.8) OR (mve > 1.5)
* Description: Buy firms with high book-to-market ratio and momentum, or firms with high market value
* Thresholds:
	+ bm: 1.1
	+ mom12m: 0.8
	+ mve: 1.5

**SELL Rule**

* Condition: (bm < -1.2 OR mom12m < -0.9) AND (mve < -1.0)
* Description: Sell firms with low book-to-market ratio and momentum, and firms with low market value
* Thresholds:
	+ bm: -1.2
	+ mom12m: -0.9
	+ mve: -1.0

**HOLD Rule**

* Condition: (bm BETWEEN -1.2 AND 1.1) AND (mom12m BETWEEN -0.9 AND 0.8) AND (mve BETWEEN -1.0 AND 1.5)
* Description: Hold firms with book-to-market ratio, momentum, and market value within the specified ranges
* Thresholds:
	+ bm: -1.2, 1.1
	+ mom12m: -0.9, 0.8
	+ mve: -1.0, 1.5

**Economic Rationale**

* Value effect: Firms with high book-to-market ratio tend to outperform the market
* Momentum effect: Firms with high momentum tend to outperform the market
* Size effect: Firms with high market value tend to outperform the market

These rules are designed to capture the value, momentum, and size effects in the stock market. The thresholds are chosen based on the historical data and are intended to provide a balance between signal strength and diversification.
