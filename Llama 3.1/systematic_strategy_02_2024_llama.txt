**Data Exploration Summary**

* The firms database contains 269,255 rows and 5 columns: `datadate`, `ticker`, `bm`, `mom12m`, and `mve`.
* The `bm` column represents the log book-to-market ratio, and values above 1 indicate undervalued firms.
* The `mom12m` column represents the 12-month momentum, and values above 0 indicate positive momentum.
* The `mve` column represents the log market value of equity, and values above 0 indicate large-cap firms.

**Explicit SELL Rule**

**HOLD Rule (Default Case)**

We will hold all firms that do not meet the buy or sell criteria.
